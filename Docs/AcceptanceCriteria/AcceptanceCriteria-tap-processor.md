## THE CARD
**TAP** — a chain stage that EMITS the stream as-it-stands at its
position AND passes it onward unchanged. One machine, layered
outputs: [ARP → TAP → HARMONIZE] = the straight walk + the
harmonized walk, simultaneously.
(Name open: TAP (desk word, short) vs SEND (the AUM ecosystem's
aux vocabulary). Paul christens.)

## PARAMS
- **LEVEL** — velocity scale on the tapped copy (the send fader).
- **TO: THIS WIRE | [A–D]** — where the tap exits. THIS WIRE =
  layering on one output (re-articulation law governs identical
  notes). A DIFFERENT wire = parallel outs (dry on A, dressed on
  B). **TO≠this makes TAP the third routing-class citizen — it
  ANNOUNCES** ("sends a copy to emitter B") per the amended
  wire-identity law.
- **MUTE** — the tap silenced without removal (A/B the layers).

## THE RENDER
The flow line BRANCHES at the tap — the snake shows the exit
(see-it-name-it; the thread forks, one arm to the wire glyph).

## COMPOSITIONS
- **Multiple taps = a layer stack**: [ARP→TAP→HARM→TAP→ECHO] =
  straight · harmonized · echoed-harmonized, three depths of one
  walk.
- **TAP vs the blend strip, the distinction stated**: blend mixes
  PREV into ONE stream (one output, one mix); TAP makes TWO
  streams (parallel outputs). Complementary, never competing.
- **TAP + PASSES v2**: the switchboard and the send compose —
  lap-alternating parallel layers.
- ECHO's DRY NOTE chip = this concept's local ancestor; TAP
  generalises it to every stage. GLIDE excluded (single-slot law).

## ENGINE NOTES
Mid-chain emission = the derived stream at that point (post-
driver: rhythm well-defined). Governor counts tapped events like
any others; refcounts/re-articulation handle same-wire overlaps;
determinism trivial. A tap occupies a chain slot (the 8-budget —
a fair price for a fork).
