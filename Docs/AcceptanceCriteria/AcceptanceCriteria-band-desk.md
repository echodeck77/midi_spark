# AcceptanceCriteria addendum — THE BAND DESK (the master corner rededicates)
_Follows the cell-station addendum; the third pointed station completes
the grammar: brush → sound · cell touch → pad · emitter touch → band._

## I — THE BAND DESK
**I0. The ruling it houses (on record)**
- Roles are INDEPENDENT — CLAIM · DUCK · ALT answer orthogonal
  questions (ownership · loudness · turns) and are never mutually
  exclusive; they compose at distinct emission-path points. CLAIM+DUCK
  is the canonical "front line" stack.
**I1. The corner's anatomy**
- Given the master corner, then it shows: a SLIM MASTER RAIL (the sum
  fader + marks · MUTE with long-press PANIC · KEY− / KEY+ · the
  REVERT and INS reserved seats — one tight column; performance
  tenants never leave the surface) beside THE ROLES AREA.
**I2. The pointer**
- When the user taps an emitter's header (strip or anywhere it
  renders), then the roles area points at that emitter (desk memory,
  like the cell station; empty state: "touch an emitter").
**I3. The good controls**
- Then the pointed emitter's roles render LARGE and LABELED: CLAIM
  (toggle · tier when it exists · LEAK % slider with value) · DUCK
  (toggle · AMOUNT slider with value; future depth/release land here)
  · ALT (toggle · COUNT; the ROTATE|DEAL axis lands here when
  summoned) — plus per-emitter OCT and channel display.
**I4. The relationship readout**
- Then one line states the emitter's social life in words, e.g.
  "OWNS 3 classes · leaks 20% · DUCKS B·C·D by 40%" — updating live.
**I5. The strips simplify**
- Given the band desk exists, the strips keep tap-toggles (lit state)
  and the vertical-drag shortcut; all labels, values, and future role
  options live in the desk. Chrome-quiet applies: unlit roles recede.
