# SPEC → Code — VELOCITY (Paul, 2026-08-20: "process velocity in
# interesting ways, performance-related" — captured, not ratified)

## THE STAGE (the roster's dynamics home; name literal per §9's
## no-metaphors law)
**MODE: SCALE | PATTERN** (the family radio; both configs persist)

- **SCALE — the dynamics compressor/expander**:
  **FLOOR · CEIL** (the output window) + **CURVE** (−1…+1: soften
  ← linear → harden). Compress a wild source into a pocket, or
  expand a flat one into life. All three MACRO-BOUND by default
  candidates — **the DYNAMICS fader**: the part breathing under
  one finger is the performance case.
- **PATTERN — the accent lane** (the drum-machine groove tool):
  the 8-slice row, states **ACCENT · PLAIN · GHOST** (levels for
  accent/ghost as two small sliders); **ROTATE** (the accent
  pattern WALKS — a punchable groove gesture); **SPAN: CELL |
  ROW** (accents inside one cell, or across the row's columns —
  the shipped chip). Pick-then-paint as everywhere.

## THE COMPOSITIONS (why it's more than its chips)
- **MOD → THIS CHAIN: VEL SCALE** (the just-specced internal
  target): a wave riding the dynamics — per-bar swells, 32-step
  crescendos over four passes — zero new machinery.
- Downstream of TUTTI/RIFF/anything: the dynamics stage shapes
  whatever arrives; upstream of ECHO: accented notes echo
  louder. Position in chain = the meaning, as ever.
- The strips' raw trim (the layer model) is UNTOUCHED — raw in
  at the console, SHAPED in the chain, performed via macros.
- Velocity-is-the-stroke: the sweep/marks render the results —
  the accent pattern becomes VISIBLE rhythm on the grid.
