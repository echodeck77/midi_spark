# AcceptanceCriteria addendum — the brush indicator + grid quieting (2026-07-30)
_Follows cells-and-colour-desk.md; same convention. From the user's
portrait critique: "it's not clear which colour you are placing."_

## E — THE BRUSH IS VISIBLE AT THE POINT OF ACTION
**E1. The verb wears the brush**
- Given a brush Colour is selected, then the PLACE button ALWAYS shows
  it: the button's fill/outline in the brush hue, the Colour's EMBLEM,
  and the label "PLACE · <NAME>" (e.g. PLACE · STRUM).
- When the brush changes (palette tap, including mid-hold), then the
  button updates immediately.
**E2. Candidates preview the brush**
- Given a PLACE hold, then pulsing empty candidates breathe IN THE
  BRUSH HUE (not neutral) — the grid previews the answer before any
  tap.
- Given a mid-hold brush switch, then the pulses AND the retro-repaint
  (existing rule) flip together in the same frame.

## F — GRID QUIETING (the chevron overload)
**F1. Watermarks recede**
- Given an empty cell at rest, then it shows NO chevron — a faint dot
  or nothing (chrome-quiet: empties recede; chevrons are reserved for
  actual controls: column keys, the rails, the desk picker caret).
- Given a PLACE hold, empties still pulse as candidates (per E2) — the
  invitation appears exactly when it's true.
**F2. One glyph, one meaning**
- Then the down-chevron appears ONLY on the column keys and the desk
  picker; the rails keep their side chevrons; no other surface borrows
  the glyph.

_Rationale on record: the brush belongs under the holding thumb (the
point of action), not at the palette (the point of selection); and an
empty cell inviting a tap that does nothing violates the honesty of
invitations. — design-side Claude_
