## THE NEW SOURCE CLASS (the bigger half)

WIRE A–D]** — a stage may read another chain's DERIVED OUTPUT.

## THE CARD — HOCKET
Sits in a chain (its door's pool = WHAT it plays); listens to a
wire (WHEN it plays). Paul's case: receiver B's material, timed
by emitter A's stream.
- **SOURCE: [WIRE A ▾ | DOOR ▾]** (the extended chip family).
- **MODE: GAPS | TRADE** —
  - GAPS: speaks only in the source's SILENCES (threshold chip:
    what counts as a gap, in fractions of a beat) — call-and-
    response between machines; the ANSWER idea's true form.
  - TRADE: hit-for-hit alternation with the source (the hocket
    proper — one line split across two synths BY LISTENING,
    the sibling of TURNS-by-dealing).
- **LENGTH: TO-NEXT | FIXED n** (ring until the source speaks
  again, or a set gate).
- The relationship framing, for the record: CLAIM/DUCK/TURNS
  made the OUTPUTS a band; HOCKET makes the CHAINS converse.
