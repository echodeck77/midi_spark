# AcceptanceCriteria — THE EMITTER PAGE (the band desk at full size)
_From the design side (DESIGN-emitter-page, 2026-08-03). PARKED: queued behind the receiver-strip DEVICE pass —
do NOT build until the strip is device-verified and the user gives the go-ahead. Captured here so it's not lost._

## 0. Status
NOT built. A future full-screen surface that houses every per-emitter wire feature (built + coming) in one
information architecture. The mode-row APPLY/CANCEL is NOT used (these are performance-adjacent, live + undoable).

## 1. ENTRY / EXIT (the two-tier grammar)
- **Short-press a role button = toggle** (unchanged, sacred).
- **LONG-PRESS a role button** = the grid area is REPLACED by THE EMITTER PAGE, pointed at that emitter, scrolled to
  that role's section (long-press DUCK → lands on DUCK). Long-press the emitter header = the page at its top.
- Strips, master, cluster STAY LIVE below (edit-page geometry: the grid is the canvas that yields). DONE (top-right)
  returns the grid. Scene switches close it.
- Header: **A · B · C · D tabs** (switch emitters without leaving; pointed = lit, each in its cyan identity) · DONE.

## 2. THE READOUT (under the tabs, always)
One live line — the emitter's social life in words: "OWNS 3 classes · leaks 20% · DUCKS B·C by 40% · turn 2 of 4".
Updates live; the page's thesis statement. Speaks only TRUE clauses.

## 3. THE SECTIONS (flat scroll · dividers · always-visible radios + sliders + chips, per the edit-page form language)
- **VOICE** — what this output IS: channel (display) · OCT · VELOCITY CURVE (floor · ceiling · curve) · MONO
  (OFF | LAST | LOW | HIGH, legato re-strike chip) · THE FENCE (range lo/hi note chips · policy DROP | CLAMP | FOLD).
- **CLAIM** — ownership: toggle · LEAK % slider · scope CLASS | RANGE (+lo/hi chips when RANGE) · release-lag chip
  (claim persists n beats after note-off).
- **DUCK** — dynamics under activity: toggle · AMOUNT slider · ATTACK / RELEASE chips (the envelope — the pumping
  feel) · TARGETS multiselect (ducks whom: B·C·D chips, default all) · note-class-filter toggle.
- **ALT** — turns: toggle · COUNT chip · ROTATE | DEAL · RING chip (ring 1|2, paired conversations) · reset-at-pass toggle.
- **FEEL** — the pocket: PUSH/LAY-BACK (± ms chip) · HUMANIZE depth (seeded, replay-safe).
- **CONVERSATION** — ensemble stance (build when sequenced; the seat exists now): LEAD ⊙ (one radio across all
  emitters) · stance FREE | WITH | AGAINST.
- **FUTURE SEATS, dimmed until built**: ECHO (per-emitter repeats) · CHOKE group · DENSITY governor. The scroll
  anticipates them; no layout rework when they land.

## 4. INTERACTION LAWS
- Changes are LIVE + undoable — NO transaction (performance-adjacent, the strip's class; the mode-row APPLY/CANCEL
  is for cell identity, not the wire).
- The strip's toggles/drags remain the quick path; the page is the labelled truth (the band-desk division, kept).
- Chrome-quiet: sections whose role is OFF render dimmed (present, legible, recessive); the readout only speaks true clauses.
