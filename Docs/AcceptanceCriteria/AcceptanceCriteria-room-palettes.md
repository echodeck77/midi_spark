## THE LAW (restated, governs everything below)
Colour's job is WAYFINDING: rooms immediately distinctive ·
door-buttons wear their DESTINATION's signature · machine hues
are local paint only (no cross-room identity burden) · marks +
glyphs ALWAYS ride alongside colour, never colour alone. The
charcoal base (current field/chrome dark) stays the constant
floor in every room. Engine colour-attribution = internal,
untouched.
