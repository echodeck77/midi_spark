## BURST gains the family radio: MODE — ONCE | COIN | PATTERN
- **ONCE** = today (fires at step/column entry). Default,
  migration-invisible.
- **COIN** = seeded chance-of-burst per step (the family's cheap
  third; offered for completeness).
- **PATTERN** = the 8-slice row, three states, pick-then-paint:
  - **BURST** — launch the roll here.
  - **CARRY** — the roll STRETCHES through this step: strikes +
    CURVE redistribute across the burst step + its contiguous
    carries (one breathing roll over the painted span; the
    "falls into a rhythm" quality, composed).
  - **REST** — silence.
  Rotate + RATE per the slice family; the window shows the spans.
- **CARRY semantics pinned**: span-stretch (strikes/curve over
  the whole span), not gate-ring — the roll's SHAPE owns the
  span; trailing note length follows the standing LENGTH laws.
- Examples for the manual: [B·C·C·R] = a long roll then air ·
  [B·R·B·C] = short hit, gap, longer spill.
— design-side Claude

  micro-composition inside one column.
- **ROW** — the slices map to THE ROW'S 8 COLUMNS: burst/carry/

  through carry-columns 2–3 (strikes + curve over the multi-

  one row, one gesture).
