# THE PAIRING CATALOG — before & after, all 31 cards (design-side, 2026-08-22)

## §0 · THE FOUR CHAIN LAWS (every pairing below obeys these)
1. **One rhythm-ruler per chain, at the head.** The whole-chord strikers (RATCHET · BURST ·
   EUCLID · STRUM · SHIFT · HUMANIZE · DRONE) and the note-walkers (ARP · CASCADE · WEAVE) all
   DRIVE. A driver mid-chain passes the set through without imprinting its rhythm — so stacking
   drivers mostly wastes one. Exception by design: **LENGTH after a driver reshapes its
   durations** (the one downstream rhythm-toucher).
2. **Set-shapers sit between the driver and the tail**: SPLIT · HARMONIZE · TUTTI (both) · CHANCE
   — they change WHAT plays, not when. Their order among themselves matters and is the fun.
3. **ECHO is the tail** (it echoes the final set — v1 ignores what's upstream of it positionally).
   **The MOD five are note-transparent**: anywhere, by convention last.
4. **GLIDE is a mono voice sink** — one emitter (v1). Since 2026-08-22 (§7①) any DRIVER may sit
   BEFORE it ([ARP→GLIDE] = the 303 line): the driver picks notes, GLIDE slides between them. Nothing
   sits AFTER (it emits bends, not notes). **PASSES gates the whole lap** — position barely matters.

## §1 · MELODY
**ARP** — BEFORE: SPLIT (walk only the top/bottom — the re-pool), TUTTI COIN (the pool flickers
one↔all: pedal-steps trading with full climbs — the classic), TUTTI PATTERN (the walk re-voiced
per slice), HARMONIZE (walk ALL the added voices — huge lines from small chords), CHANCE (a
thinned pool per loop). AFTER: LENGTH (gate-override: staccato/tie the walk — the canon pairing),
SPLIT (punch holes: non-subset visits become rests), CHANCE (drop steps), HARMONIZE (chord-stab
every step), ECHO (trailing walks), any MOD.
**CASCADE** — BEFORE: SPLIT (build only part of the chord), TUTTI PATTERN (reveal different
shapes). AFTER: HARMONIZE (each joining note blooms into voices — the swelling-pad build), LENGTH
(clip the reveals), ECHO (each arrival echoes), MOD ENVELOPE (a filter swell riding the build).
**STRUM** — BEFORE: SPLIT (rake a register), TUTTI COIN (rakes alternating with single plucks),
HARMONIZE (thicker rakes). AFTER: LENGTH (mute-slice the rake's tail), ECHO (rakes that trail),
CHANCE (broken rakes). Note: STRUM's stagger only imprints from the head (law 1).
**GLIDE** — BEFORE (v2, LANDED 2026-08-22): any DRIVER — [ARP→GLIDE] (the 303 line: stepped acid
slides), [CASCADE→GLIDE] (a rising contour dissolves into one gliding voice), [EUCLID/BURST/RATCHET/
STRUM/WEAVE→GLIDE] (rhythmic material becomes a mono slide). The driver picks the notes; GLIDE slides
between them — in-range steps bend over TIME, leaps past RANGE re-anchor (or clamp). AFTER: none —
GLIDE is a mono voice sink (it emits bends, not a note set). v1: single emitter; GLIDE is mono, so a
set-shaper between the driver and GLIDE is ignored (fold-through is a v2 item).

## §2 · HARMONY
**HARMONIZE** — BEFORE: ARP/CASCADE/WEAVE (expand each emitted note), SPLIT (voice only the kept
part), TUTTI COIN (solo steps get voiced — instant block-harmony moments). AFTER: ARP (walk the
expanded set), SPLIT (keep only the voicing's top), CHANCE (voices flicker), ECHO. Order flip is
the lesson: [HARM→ARP] walks everything; [ARP→HARM] stabs chords per step.
**TUTTI COIN** — BEFORE: SPLIT (coin over a register), HARMONIZE (the "chord" side is richer).
AFTER: ARP (the classic — pedal↔climb), STRUM (pluck↔rake), RATCHET (single hits vs full rolls),
HARMONIZE (solo notes get dressed), ECHO.
**TUTTI PATTERN** — BEFORE: HARMONIZE/SPLIT (shape a richer or narrower set). AFTER: ARP (comping
built into the walk), any striker (the authored shapes become its material), LENGTH (phrase the
shapes), ECHO.
**SPLIT** — BEFORE: HARMONIZE (then keep a slice of the voicing). AFTER: everything — SPLIT is the
great pre-filter: [SPLIT→ARP] [SPLIT→RATCHET] [SPLIT→WEAVE] all mean "this register only." The
flip [driver→SPLIT] = rhythm kept, holes punched.
**DRONE** — chain-mode only here (per-column pad re-strike). BEFORE: SPLIT (pad a register),
HARMONIZE (wider pad). AFTER: LENGTH (shape the pad per slice), MOD LFO (the pad breathes), ECHO
(washes). Alone in a slot = the continuous hold (its other life).

## §3 · RHYTHM
**RATCHET / COIN / PATTERN** — BEFORE: SPLIT (roll a register), TUTTI COIN (rolls vs single hits
per step), HARMONIZE (thicker stabs), CHANCE (thinned stabs). AFTER: LENGTH (choke or ring the
rolls), CHANCE (crackle — some repeats drop), ECHO (rolls that trail — dense fast), VELOCITY when
it lands (accent the rolls). The COIN/PATTERN cards pair identically; their difference is when
rolls happen, not what pairs.
**BURST / COIN / PATTERN** — BEFORE: SPLIT · HARMONIZE · TUTTI PATTERN (shape what the roll
strikes). AFTER: LENGTH (the roll's ring shaped — with PATTERN's CARRY this is the composed-roll
kit), ECHO (fireworks), MOD ENVELOPE (a sweep per burst).
**EUCLID** — BEFORE: SPLIT (euclid a register — kick-and-hat splits from one chord), TUTTI PATTERN
(per-hit shapes), HARMONIZE. AFTER: LENGTH (gate the pulses), CHANCE (drop hits — evolving
euclid), ECHO (dub pulses), HUMANIZE? no — law 1 (it drives); use MOD instead.
**WEAVE (all four)** — BEFORE: SPLIT (weave fewer voices — essential on big chords), HARMONIZE
(denser lattice — careful, the governor watches). AFTER: LENGTH (phrase the lattice), CHANCE (a
breathing weave — drop pulses), SPLIT (mute a rank's holes), ECHO (shimmer — keep FADE steep).
The four mode-cards pair identically.
**PASSES** — anywhere, with anything: the lap gate composes with every chain ([PASSES→X] and
[X→PASSES] sound the same). Star use: two cells, complementary lap masks, alternating machines.
**CHANCE** — BEFORE a driver: thins the pool (fewer voices all bar). AFTER a driver: drops events
(broken rhythm, loop-stable). Both musics are real; teach the flip. Pairs with everything except
GLIDE.

## §4 · DYNAMICS
**HUMANIZE** — a feel DRIVER (law 1): it strikes the chord itself. BEFORE: SPLIT/HARMONIZE/TUTTI
(shape its material). AFTER: LENGTH · CHANCE · ECHO · MOD. Not after other drivers.
**SHIFT** — same shape as HUMANIZE (a laid-back striker): BEFORE: set-shapers. AFTER: LENGTH ·
ECHO (behind-the-beat echoes are lovely) · MOD.
**VELOCITY SCALE / PATTERN (when they land)** — AFTER any driver (shape its dynamics; PATTERN's
accents before ECHO = accented notes echo louder — the canon). BEFORE ECHO always; after
everything else.

## §5 · CONTROL (LFO · FOLLOWER · STEP MOD · ENVELOPE · CC IN)
Note-transparent: pair with EVERYTHING, position free, convention last. The star pairings are
TARGET choices, not chain order: LFO→filter under a WEAVE lattice · FOLLOWER→reverb-send against
density (busy = dry, the classic inverse) · ENVELOPE per cell entry over CASCADE builds · STEP MOD
locked to the row (SPAN ROW) under RATCHET PATTERN — the two 8-slice rows in lockstep · CC IN
re-ranging a hardware knob onto anything. Internal-target mode (THIS CHAIN) pairs with its own
chain by definition: LFO on ARP's LENGTH = breathing gates.

## §6 · TIME
**ECHO** — AFTER everything (the tail law). Best sources: sparse drivers (EUCLID low-K, BURST ONCE,
single TUTTI-COIN solos); dense sources need low REPEATS/steep FADE (the governor is watching).
BEFORE: nothing benefits (v1 echoes the final set).
**LENGTH** — the great AFTER: reshapes any driver's durations downstream (the only stage that
may). BEFORE a driver = it re-articulates its own slices instead (standalone music). Star:
[ARP→LENGTH] · [RATCHET→LENGTH] (choked rolls) · [WEAVE→LENGTH].
**SHIFT** — listed in §4 (it drives).

## RATIFICATION (Paul, 2026-08-22): ① and ② are now BUILD ORDERS. ③ (PASSES v2) stays captured, unratified.
① **LANDED 2026-08-22 — GLIDE AFTER A MONO DRIVER — v2.** ARP (and CASCADE) emit exactly what GLIDE
consumes: a mono stream. [ARP→GLIDE] = the 303 line — steps bend over glideTime, leaps re-anchor per
TOO FAR, rests end the phrase. Built generically across ALL drivers (the driver's notes feed GLIDE's
mono voice via emitGlideDriven; §1 GLIDE gains its first real entries below). v1: single emitter —
multi-emitter fan-out stays a separate v2 item. macOS 827 green; device ear owed.
② **RATIFIED — ECHO MID-CHAIN — the existing birthstone stands ready.** The ROUTE CHAIN|DIRECT chip
(unrendered, by design) is this exact future: echoes flowing THROUGH downstream stages = sculpted
trails ([ECHO→LENGTH]: choked repeats · [ECHO→SPLIT]: trails thinned to a register). D7's
position-blindness is v1, not law.
③ **PASSES v2 — THE PER-LAP SWITCHBOARD** (the relic reborn; per Paul: "different routing on
different passes"). Per pass (1–4), a state: **PASS** (downstream receives) · **MUTE** (the lap
rests) · **BYPASS** (skip the remaining chain this lap — the chain shortens on alternate laps:
timbral alternation, one card) · **TO [wire]** (this lap exits at the gate to a chosen emitter —
**ALT at lap grain**; routing-class, so it ANNOUNCES per the amended wire-identity law, TURNS'
younger sibling). Position now carries full meaning: the card's seat = where the fork happens.
[ARP→PASSES(BYPASS on 2,4)→HARM→ECHO] = dressed laps trading with naked ones. MOD-last stays a
convention only ✓ as written.
