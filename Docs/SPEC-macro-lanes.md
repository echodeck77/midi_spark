## The band
A COLLAPSIBLE strip below the PART grid: per-part **MACRO
LANES** (lean: up to 4, v1). Each lane:
- **TARGET**: MACRO 1–8 (the offset engine, base ⊕ Σ — no new
  modulation class; MEAN/last-writer laws stand).
- **THE CURVE**: the ratified SLIDER-LANE widget (drawn per
  column) **or a WAVE chip** (SINE · TRI · RAMP · SQUARE + a
  rate) — hand-drawn or LFO-styled, one toggle.
- **SPAN**: the ladder — 16 (the row) · ×2 · ×4 = **MULTI-PASS
  SWEEPS** ("LFO-style passes": a filter opening over four laps,
  drawn once). Anchored at row origin; the lap indicator marks
  the live segment.
- Boundary-deferred application; drawn = config (deterministic);
  saved with the part; the e-brush legal on lanes as everywhere.

## Kinship (no dupes)
MOD's STEP/lanes = CHAIN-scoped (a stage's modulation). These =
PART-scoped, chain-agnostic — the part's own automation strip.
Siblings, distinct grains, one shared widget. The parked
AUTOMATION SWEEP (one-way A→B) remains its own future; a RAMP
wave at ×4 covers most of its want meanwhile.
