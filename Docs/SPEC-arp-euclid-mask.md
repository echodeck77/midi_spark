## THE CONTROLS (one mask; the fence is the feature)
- **HITS: ◀K▶ of ◀N▶** — the nudge grammar; **default K = N**
  ("8 of 8") = today's arp exactly. OFF is not a state; it's the
  default's absence — at K=N the pair renders DIMMED
  (defaults-recede, idea 21) and everything below is NOT PRESENT.
- The moment K < N, the kit ANIMATES IN (idea 25):
  - **GAPS: REST | TIE** — non-hit steps are silence, or sustain
    the previous note (TIE = varying lengths from a constant
    walk; with GLIDE SYNTH downstream, tied steps SLIDE — acid
    phrasing from two nudge pairs).
  - **WALK: MARCH | WAIT** — march = the pattern position
    advances through rests (holes punched in the line); wait =
    the walk steps only on hits (the full sequence, re-spaced).
  - **ROTATE: ◀ n ▶** — the ratified pair.

## THE SCOPE LAW (decided in-session, on record)
ONE mask only. Separate/composing REST + TIE masks are REFUSED —
two interacting masks need a collision law and un-attributable
ears (you tweak one, hear the other). Mixed per-step rest/tie
phrasing is AUTHORED territory and already has two homes: LENGTH's
matrix and RIFF's tie lane. The arp's version stays discoverable:
turn K down, hear phrasing, done.

## Engine notes
Seeded-free (the mask is deterministic Bjorklund, same as EUCLID);
WAIT = advance-on-hit only (the walk index gates on the mask);
MARCH = index free-runs. TIE emits no note-on (gate extension per
the standing LENGTH laws). K=N short-circuits to the untouched
path (byte-identity cheap to test).
