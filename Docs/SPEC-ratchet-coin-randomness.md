## 2 · REFIRE GAP (Paul's — the phrasing control)
**GAP: 0–4 steps** — after a fire, the coin cannot fire again for
N steps. Clusters die; rolls breathe. (Its sibling, a MAX-STREAK
cap, folds in here if ever wanted — same family, opposite end.)

## 3 · QUOTA (the density promise)
**PER ROW: FREE | ~2 | ~3 | ~4** — the dice constrained to a
rough budget per row: predictable density, surprising placement.
The onset-budget idea at coin grain; seeded, replay-safe.

## 4 · ODDS FROM: FIXED | VELOCITY (the performance coupling)
Fire-odds scale with the incoming note's velocity — play harder,
more rolls. The randomness becomes playable; soft passages stay
clean, accents erupt. One chip.
